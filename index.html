FAQ

Quick answers

What does "unlimited" mean?

Every usable photo from your session is individually edited, with no cap and no upsell.

Where do shoots happen?

Most shoots happen at a beautiful park. The Botanic Garden in the city and the Japanese garden are fan favourites, but another location can work just as well. Anywhere within 10km of the Adelaide CBD is at no extra cost, and further locations can be arranged.

How do I pay?

Payment is due before your photoshoot day. PayID or cash is preferred, and we also accept Visa and Mastercard if needed.

Can I reschedule?

We are not able to offer rescheduling, so please pick a date you are confident about. Cancellations are not accepted within 48 hours of your shoot.

What should I wear?

Choose comfortable clothes you feel confident in. Solid colours and simple textures usually photograph best, so go easy on tiny busy patterns and large logos. You are welcome to bring one spare outfit for a second look, just keep in mind that changing will use up some of your 1-hour session.
